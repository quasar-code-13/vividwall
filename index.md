# Vividwall — Support

**Live video wallpapers for your Mac.**

---

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I set a wallpaper?**
Select a wallpaper in the sidebar, then click a display chip in the detail pane — or use "Play on all monitors". Click an active chip again to stop it on that display.

**How do I add my own videos?**
Click *Add Video* (or press `⌘O`), or simply drag video files anywhere onto the Vividwall window. MP4, MOV, M4V, AVI, and MPEG are supported.

**Can each display show a different wallpaper?**
Yes — pick any wallpaper for any display. Each video also has its own playback speed (0.25×–2×), and every display showing that video stays in sync.

**Does it drain my battery?**
Vividwall is built to be polite: Battery Guard pauses playback when your battery runs low, and playback also pauses in Low Power Mode, when your Mac runs warm, and when full-screen apps cover the desktop. All of this is configurable in Settings.

**I deleted a built-in wallpaper. Can I get it back?**
Yes — open Settings and click *Restore Built-in Wallpapers*.

**Where are my videos stored?**
Imported videos are copied into Vividwall's own library inside the app's sandbox container. Your original files are never modified or moved.

**What does Vividwall require?**
A Mac running macOS 13 (Ventura) or later.
